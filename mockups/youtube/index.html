YouTube thumbnails

The Seeds of Renaissance design system applied to four kinds of video, shown full size and then at real size in a YouTube feed, in light and dark mode.

How the system carries over

  • Ink, paper and white as the ground, with one dark (night) ground used sparingly, as on the website.
  • Polyamine for titles, kept to two to six words so they read at feed size; Appful Grotesque for small labels.
  • The torn edge joins photo and paper, the signature device from the website.
  • Colour comes from photographs. No red in the type: links are the only red on the website, and a thumbnail has no links. The logo brings its own red.
  • Paper grain is kept very light, so it doesn't turn to noise when YouTube compresses the image.

Titles are samples. Photos are mood board placeholders.
