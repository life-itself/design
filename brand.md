# Seeds of Renaissance — Brand

*Single source for the brand's substance: why, what, for whom. **No visual design here:** no palette, type or layout; that lives in the [design system](README.md). How we write: [voice.md](voice.md).*

## One line

**Tagline: Vision and movement for civilisational rebirth.**

Hero line: *For those who sense the old world is ending and want to help seed the new.*

**Essence: a movement and a community.** Media (podcast, magazine, essays) is how people find us and make sense. Belonging and practice together are what we are. The site should feel like joining a living movement of people, not subscribing to a publication.

Dedication (from the manifesto): *For all the living beings whose heart burns to see a new world emerge.*

## Our ambition

The birth of a **new cultural paradigm**: one that pursues wisdom, spirituality and interbeing, and contributes to the evolution of consciousness itself. In short, a new way of being human.

## Why we are here

We are living in a moment of transition. An old civilisation (modernity) is slowly dying, and the new world has not yet been born. There is widespread disillusionment and loss of meaning, but no cultural vision yet coherent enough to guide what comes next.

**One crisis, not many.** The ecological, political, social and spiritual crises are not separate emergencies to manage one by one. They are symptoms of a civilisation whose foundations are cracking. As in every great turning, the crisis carries within it the seed of rebirth: a *second renaissance*.

## What we do

Seeds of Renaissance is a **media house and a set of spaces of belonging**. We offer mythos and language for what people already feel, together with the ideas, the practice and the company to help birth the new. We are a movement that creates greenhouses for a new paradigm.

- **Vision**: a coherent, larger story that tells the truth about the moment and opens toward something worth moving toward. It draws on philosophy, art, tradition and spiritual practice.
- **Practice**: embodied proof that another way is possible, lived out in farmhouses, studios, rituals, conversations and courses, not just argued in essays.
- **Community**: the company of others doing the same work, without pretending anyone has it figured out.

It is **a constellation, not a programme.** People share an orientation, each finds their own expression of it, and those expressions become a garden of many forms.

### Offerings

**Media House** (discover, engage, make sense)

- **Podcast / YouTube**: one brand, *Seeds of Renaissance*, on one channel and one podcast feed. Kinds of episode (interviews, Rufus–Sylvie conversations, later perhaps *Temple of the Sacred World* material) are formats shown by playlist and artwork tag, not separate shows. *Over the Mountains* is retired as a show name ([why](https://github.com/life-itself/planning/blob/main/projects/2608-seeds-of-renaissance-rebrand.md#appendix-a-podcast-naming-one-brand-2026-10-05)). The channel is where the voices meet in motion.
- **Magazine**: a yearly art-led print periodical where artists, writers and philosophers gather. #1 *Impermanence* (2024), #2 *Mythos* (2026).
- **Essays and white papers**: long-form, rigorous thinking on the moment and what comes next.
- **Manifesto**: the vision and principles of the movement, plus the art collective.
- **Greenhouse Newsletter**: releases, deeper thematic paths, events and opportunities.

**Gardens of Change** (belong, practise, create)

- **Courses and events**: practices people can take into their lives, plus in-person gatherings.
- **Community gardens**: online groups and meetups.
- **Local Gardens of Change**: people seeding hubs where they live.

**The journey:** you sense something isn't right → you search for words → you find us (podcast, essays, magazine) → you find belonging → you practise → you seed something where you are.

## What makes us different

- **Theory grounded in lived practice.** We walk the talk. Our hope is authentic and honest because it comes from real life, not a pitch.
- **Alchemy.** We hold together intellect and art, masculine and feminine, spirituality and rigour, darkness and hope, contemplation and action. This shows in the stewards themselves: Sylvie Shiwei Barbier (ritual artist) and Rufus Pollock (researcher and writer).
- **One organism, many expressions.** This is not audience segmentation. The magazine can lean poetic and the papers rigorous, but the integration is the identity.
- **Inner and outer work together.**

## Who we are here for

People who:

- are disillusioned by the crises of modern civilisation
- sense that something new is trying to emerge
- want to take part in bringing forth a new paradigm
- seek meaning, depth, wisdom and beauty
- are intellectually curious and also practically hungry, looking for grounded hope

Some already know these ideas conceptually but not how to live them. Others feel isolated, overwhelmed or cynical after watching movements fail or lose integrity.

**Entry point is the sensing, not the conclusion.** We speak to people who feel the gap before they can name it.

Personas:

- **Meaning seeker** (mid-30s–50s): therapist, teacher, tech, health, consultant. Reads McGilchrist, listens to Hagens and Vervaeke. Aesthetically sensitive; wants the people they follow to have done the reading.
- **Post-disillusionment renewer** (40s–60s): ex-church, ex-corporate, ex-academic, ex-activist. Allergic to hype. Trusts people who admit they don't have it figured out.
- **Awakening professional**: successful and feels the system is off. Hasn't found their people. Has little time, so needs clear, digestible pieces.
- **Disillusioned young seeker** (18–30): searching for meaning, belonging, a larger vision and real community. Values authenticity.

What they want to feel: *"These people are honest. They're living it. I'm not alone in seeing what I see."*

**Not for** the cynical or given-up, who aren't seeking.

## What we value

Depth, rigour, wisdom and taking the long view. Integration of theory and practice in service of a larger vision. An artistic, embodied quality. Doing both the inner and the outer work.

## Personality

**Authentic, grounded hope, with depth.** In short: **honest, rigorous hope**. Neither doom nor naive optimism. It is hope that has done the hard thinking and still opens toward something.

## Voice and register

**Poet-philosopher and lucid thinker.** The lyrical leads and rigour grounds it. We speak with kind authority and gentle curiosity, **from within** the crisis rather than above it, and we hold grief and possibility at once. People should feel held, not lectured. Register varies by place and is set deliberately: plain on entry pages, poetic in the manifesto, magazine and art, rigorous in essays and papers, conversational on the podcast. The working guide, with key lines, register by place and words: [voice.md](voice.md).

**Avoid:** cold intellectualism, activist urgency, hype, insider jargon on entry pages, wellness or spiritual-lifestyle gloss, corporate polish.

Themes: inquisitive, curious, confident, embodied, optimistic, real, rigorous.

Public descriptions of the podcast's formats go by **host and format**. Framing like "masculine/feminine" stays internal.
