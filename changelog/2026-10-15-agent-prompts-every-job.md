[Use with an agent](https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org/prompts) now has a prompt to paste into your AI agent for each common job: a podcast episode cover, slides (presented from the browser, with a PDF), a diagram, a paper typeset as a PDF, finding the right logo or graphic, and changing the design system itself. Each guide ([Podcast](https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org/podcast), [Slides](https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org/slides), [Diagrams](https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org/diagrams), [Print](https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org/print)) opens with its prompt.
