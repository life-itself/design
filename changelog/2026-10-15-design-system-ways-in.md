title: The Seeds of Renaissance design system has a new home page and navigation
promote: true
---

The [design system](https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org) now opens with three ways in: making something (pick the format to get its guide, an example to copy and a Canva kit where there is one), getting a file (logo, graphics, photos, fonts), or understanding the brand. The navigation follows the same lines: Start here, Make, Assets, Foundations, Brand and Examples. The guides are shorter and say only how things are done now, and the gallery thumbnails now show on the live site.
