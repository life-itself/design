The Seeds of Renaissance design system now sets out how our papers look and are put together, drawn page by page from *Wisdom and Wanting What's Good*: a typographic cover, title page and imprint, annotated contents, chapter openings, body pages with plates, pull quotes and footnotes, and the back cover. Each paper ships as one PDF for reading on screen. [See the print guide](https://sor-design-system-rufuspollock.flowershow.me/print).
