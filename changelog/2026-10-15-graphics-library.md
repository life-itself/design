Every graphic in the Seeds of Renaissance design system is now in one library: woodcuts and prints, animals, painted botanicals and washes, line figures and marks, mountains and the brand marks, each set with a card on one page and its own gallery on any ground it may use. One rules table says, for every set, which colours, grounds and registers it suits and whether its rights are cleared. See the [graphics library](https://design.seedsofrenaissance.org/graphics).
