# Directions — draft 1

*2026-09-28. Four deliberately different directions for the look and feel. They come from [brand.md](../brand.md) and the [reference review](../moodboard/README.md), not from existing 2R or Canva design. Colour and type notes here are fresh proposals to test, not decisions.*

**Correction (Rufus, 2026-09-28):** the references are mainly for **fonts and feel**, and several are magazines. SoR's essence is **vision and movement for civilisational renewal**: a movement and community first, with media as the way in. So in every direction:

- **Home leads with people and belonging**, not content: faces, gatherings, voices, "you're not alone", and clear ways to join (come to a gathering, take a course, join the community, start a Garden of Change). Media (latest episode, essay, issue) sits in support.
- **Movement signals** are first-class components: upcoming gatherings, where people are (local gardens, a constellation or map), participant voices, founders present as people, and a simple "how to take part" path.
- The magazine is one expression, not the model for the whole site. This raises the **generic-magazine risk for A**.

Each direction answers the same question differently: **how does one living world hold the artist and the philosopher, the poetic and the lucid, together?**

---

## A. Living Magazine

**Quiet frame, luminous content.** A literary magazine that is also a movement. The frame steps back so that artworks, photos and words lead.

- **From:** Emergence (quiet frame, per-piece artwork, labelled forms, editorial statement) + Openhouse (breathing asymmetric grid, documentary photography, serif italics).
- **Type:** a classical book serif with a beautiful italic for headings and long reading, plus a calm grotesk for UI, labels and practical information.
- **Colour:** paper-like light ground and near-black ink. Colour comes almost entirely from the imagery. Each issue, station or series can carry its own single accent.
- **Imagery:** large and full-bleed. Documentary photography of people and places, plus artworks given room like plates in a book.
- **Layout:** generous margins, a staggered two-column list of pieces, content labelled by form (ESSAY · POEM · PODCAST · COURSE).
- **Motion:** minimal. Slow fades.
- **Home:** a full-bleed photo of people gathered with the one-line hero, a short "what we believe" statement, then ways to take part and upcoming gatherings, with the latest media below.
- **Manifesto:** a reading page with a great italic serif, and the 21 lines as a quiet litany.
- **Magazine:** the issue as an object, spreads and a contributor list.
- **Essay:** excellent long read with sidenotes.
- **Strengths:** warm, trustworthy, matures well, and reads beautifully. It suits all personas.
- **Risk:** it could be "tasteful editorial" that looks like many others: a magazine, not a movement. It needs one signature element to be ours.

## B. Studio Index

**Made by artists, not a brand agency.** Raw, type-driven and alive: an index of a living practice.

- **From:** Cargo's artist sites (index layouts, publications as objects, freedom to be strange) + Lee Holmes (old letterforms in a new medium, images resolving, big plain statement).
- **Type:** one striking display face with manuscript or engraving ancestry but a contemporary cut (the renaissance gesture: old form, new medium), a monospace for meta and labels, and a plain grotesk for reading.
- **Colour:** mostly black and white, with **one signal colour** used sparingly as a live wire.
- **Imagery:** in index grids, engravings and plates, publications photographed as objects. Images **resolve from coarse to clear**: the seed becoming.
- **Layout:** lists and indexes (numbered works, dated entries), dense in places and empty in others. It reads like a studio wall.
- **Motion:** purposeful quirks: text resolving, images sharpening, cursor traces.
- **Home:** a large plain-spoken statement paragraph, then a live index of the movement: gatherings, gardens and people, alongside episodes and essays, all with dates and places.
- **Manifesto:** the 21 lines as a numbered index, one per line, very large.
- **Magazine:** the issue as art object, with spreads in a grid.
- **Essay:** a plain, precise reading column with numbered premises.
- **Strengths:** unmistakably contemporary and alive. Speaks to disillusioned young seekers and artists. Nobody in the space looks like this.
- **Risk:** can feel cool, cold or exclusive to the 40–60 renewers, and readability suffers if pushed too far.

## C. Honest Workshop

**Plainly human.** The site is a well-kept notebook from people doing the work: clear, warm, unpolished on purpose.

- **From:** ECC (plain listings: who, what, when, fee, how to join; the big testimonial as the emotional moment) + Emergence's editorial statement.
- **Type:** one humanist sans for nearly everything, plus a large italic (serif or sans) reserved for voices: quotes, testimonials, manifesto lines.
- **Colour:** a warm off-white ground, dark text and one earthy accent. Very restrained.
- **Imagery:** real photos of real gatherings, kitchens, fields and hands, unretouched. Few artworks.
- **Layout:** a single column, clear sections, practical information given first-class treatment.
- **Motion:** none.
- **Home:** "For those who sense the old world is ending…", a short honest paragraph, then what's on (next course, next gathering, latest episode) listed plainly, then big-italic voices from participants.
- **Manifesto:** simple and direct, with its power left to the words.
- **Magazine:** a plain product page. It undersells the art.
- **Essay:** a clean reading column.
- **Strengths:** maximally honest and "we don't pretend", with the lowest hype. Fast to build and easy to maintain.
- **Risk:** it loses beauty, myth and the artist half of the alchemy, and can look amateur. Probably better as a **component** (how offers are presented) than as the whole identity.

## D. Dusk & Dawn

**The threshold made visible.** The site moves between night and day: the mystical and the lucid as two lights of one world.

- **From:** brand.md (the alchemy, the dying and the being born, the register split) with Emergence's ceremony and Lee Holmes's old-in-new, in a restrained form.
- **Idea:** two coordinated modes. **Dusk** (a deep, dark ground) is for manifesto, magazine, ritual and podcast: the poetic and mystical register. **Dawn** (a light, clear ground) is for home, courses, essays and about: the lucid register. Moving between them is part of the experience, and the home page can hold both, perhaps as a gradient across the scroll from dark to light.
- **Type:** a display serif with some mythic weight (carved or inscriptional feel) for dusk, and a precise text face for dawn. They share a family or a clear kinship.
- **Colour:** built around a deep night tone and a clear dawn light, with one warm accent (fire or first light) bridging them. **Fresh proposal to test, not inherited from any brand doc.**
- **Imagery:** at dusk, archival plates (eclipse, the Doré sun), fire, night gatherings, B&W photography. At dawn, daylight documentary photography.
- **Motion:** slow light transitions: gradients, the page "brightening" as you scroll toward invitation.
- **Home:** opens at dusk ("something is ending") and scrolls into dawn ("something is being born"), where it arrives at people, gatherings and ways to join. The brand narrative becomes the scroll.
- **Manifesto:** full dusk, a litany in light type on dark.
- **Magazine:** dusk.
- **Essay:** dawn.
- **Strengths:** the most **ownable** concept. It carries the thesis (death and rebirth) and the register split in the design itself, and has a strong signature.
- **Risk:** it can tip into mystical cliché (dark plus stars plus gold). Dark long-reads are tiring, so essays must stay dawn. It is also harder to build well.

## Comparison

| | A. Living Magazine | B. Studio Index | C. Honest Workshop | D. Dusk & Dawn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Feels like | Emergence/Openhouse | Artist studio | A trusted notebook | A threshold |
| Artist ↔ philosopher | Balanced, literary | Artist-leaning | Practitioner-leaning | Both, as two modes |
| Ownability | Medium | High | Low | High |
| Warmth | High | Low–medium | High | Medium |
| Long-form reading | Excellent | Good | Good | Good (dawn) |
| Personas | All | Young seekers, artists | Renewers, practitioners | Meaning seekers, all |
| Risk | Generic-tasteful | Cold/exclusive | Amateur, no myth | Cliché |

## Suggestion

- **Mock A, B and D** as full directions.
- **Fold C into all of them** as the pattern for practical offers (courses, events, subscriptions): plain who, what, when and cost, plus a big voice quote.
- For each direction: **home + manifesto** (the two that differ most), then magazine and essay for the finalist.
- **Hybrids are likely.** For example A's reading and imagery with D's dusk/dawn structure, or A with one B-style signature display face.
