- **Pomegranates** (`pomegranates-linocut`): Two pomegranates, one whole and one cut open, as a two-colour linocut: black key block over a flat red that sits slightly out of register. Source: Pinterest linocut.
- **Oak leaf and acorn on a twig** (`oak-leaf-acorn-twig-linocut`): One oak leaf and one acorn on a sweeping twig. Solid black, thin cut veins, cut-out acorn cup. Source: Pinterest linocut.
- **Acorn** (`acorn-single-linocut`): A single acorn with a ribbed nut, speckled cap and stray gouge marks around the edge. Source: Stock linocut set (watermarked, draft).
- **Oak leaf, down-right** (`oak-leaf-linocut-a`): An oak leaf angled down and to the right, with white cut veins and stray cuts. Source: Stock linocut set (watermarked, draft).
- **Oak leaf, up-right** (`oak-leaf-linocut-b`): An oak leaf angled up and to the right, with white cut veins and stray cuts. Source: Stock linocut set (watermarked, draft).
- **Two acorns with an oak leaf** (`acorns-pair-oak-leaf-linocut`): A pair of acorns with crosshatched cups and a small oak leaf above. Source: Stock linocut set (watermarked, draft).
- **Pinecone, bold** (`pinecone-linocut`): A tilted pinecone in strong black and white, with chunky outlined scales that go ragged at the tip. Source: Pinterest linocut.
- **Pinecone hanging from a twig** (`pinecone-hanging-twig-pen`): A pinecone hanging from a pine twig with long, fine needles. A thin pen drawing, best used large. Source: Pinterest pen drawing.
- **Hanging fruit and leaves** (`hanging-fruit-silhouette`): Two round fruit with leaf clusters as a cut-paper silhouette. Thin white gaps do the drawing. Vertical, so it suits a margin or divider. Source: Craft-shop stencil (draft).
- **Round flowers on a branching stem** (`round-flowers-stem-ink`): Pom-pom flower heads on a long, thin branching stem. Light and airy, with heavy dots on a hairline. Source: Pinterest ink drawing.
- **Tulips in loose brush line** (`tulips-brush`): Five tulip-like flowers in loose, tapering brush strokes. Source image was AI-generated, so mood reference. Source: Pinterest (AI-generated).
- **Lily stem** (`lily-stem-brush`): A lily stem in a few swelling brush lines, with leaves left partly open. Source: Pinterest ink study.
- **Wildflowers** (`wildflowers-pen`): Three wildflowers and two buds in scratchy pen line with dark centres. Clip-art crop, so a draft. Source: Pinterest clip-art preview (draft).
- **Flowering branch, cropped** (`flowering-branch-silhouette`): A flowering branch as a flat silhouette, cropped so it runs off the frame. Works as a background or tile. Source: Pinterest silhouette.
- **Mountain range** (`mountain-range-linocut`, with or without `-sun`): a range of three peaks in strong black and white with cut strokes for the plain below; the sun versions add a small gold sun. Source: Pinterest linocut with gold leaf.
- **Flock of swallows** (`swallows-flock-engraving`): about eleven barn swallows rising in a loose diagonal flock, in fine engraving line. Our main motif. Large files (about 650 KB each) because of the hatching; use them large. Source: Pinterest engraving.
