# Logo (proposed)

A black swallow at the centre of a radiating circle of red fingerprints, on white.

## Origin and flexibility

- The red is literally the red of the original ink handprints used to make it. It was not chosen as a brand colour and **can be adjusted**.
- The logo works on different grounds: on black with the swallow inverted, and so on. That flexibility is one of its strengths.
- **The logo's colours are not necessarily the site palette.** This is an open question (see the plan's colour section).

## What it carries

- **Grounded hope, literally:** many human hands (ground, body, community) around a bird in flight (hope, the announcement of spring).
- **Texture and imperfection:** fingerprints are hand-made, unique and irregular. This is a native texture the wider system can draw on.
- **Wholeness:** the circle (Christopher Alexander).
- **Liminality:** the rings radiate like a sunrise or a birth, with the swallow breaking out from the centre.

## Issues to address

1. **The red is a clean, bright screen red.** A deeper, ink-like red (real stamp or printing ink) may fit the earthy, slightly muddy feel better. Test a few.
2. **The swallow is a common stock silhouette.** A hand-drawn or printmade swallow would be more distinctive and match the brand's craft.
3. **It fails at small sizes.** On a favicon or YouTube avatar the fingerprints turn into pink noise. We need a simplified version, for example the swallow alone or the swallow in a solid ring.
4. **We need a set of versions:** full colour, one colour (black on light, light on dark), inverted, and small.

## Liked so far

- The logo set in a **white disc** (footer, avatars) or a **white square tile**. It gives the mark more dynamism and lets it sit on any ground, including night.
