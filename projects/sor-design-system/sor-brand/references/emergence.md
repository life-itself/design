# Reference: Emergence Magazine

- URL: https://emergencemagazine.org
- Studied: 2026-10-01 (home page)
- Status: **close but not quite.** Too nature-focused, woo-woo and sleek-magazine.
- Caveat: the page's animated reveal didn't render in the browser used, so this study is based on the page text, structure and computed styles, plus general knowledge of the site. The imagery should be reviewed by eye.

## Summary

A spare, white, content-first magazine site. It has a clear structure and a strong editorial voice, with large photography and film. It is rigorous in structure but sleek and clean, close to luxury, and it has no hand or texture.

## Observed details

### Type
- **Sans for text:** GT America.
- **Typewriter face for labels:** Courier Prime Sans.
- **Wordmark:** a soft, rounded display serif ("Emergence Magazine").
- Section headings are small uppercase labels (about 18px): "VOLUME 7: SEEDS AND STRUCTURES", "EDITORIAL STATEMENT", "SELECTED STORIES", "ENGAGE". Story titles are about 24px.

### Colour
| Use | Value |
|---|---|
| Main background | `#ffffff`, `#fcfcfc` |
| Warm off-white section | `#f6f5ef` |
| Dark sections | `#000000` |
| Cards | translucent greys (black at 5%, grey at 10%) |
| Text | black, and white on dark |

Almost no interface colour. Colour comes from photography and film.

### Structure
- **Feature** at the top (50 Seasonal Meditations).
- **The print edition** with price and pre-order (Volume 7: Seeds and Structures).
- **Editorial statement**, a short paragraph of purpose: sharing stories in dark times as a radical act.
- **Selected stories, sorted by medium:** Read / Listen / Watch, each item labelled by type (Poem, Essay, Conversation, Feature) with author.
- **Engage:** events (for example a launch at the Architectural Association, London, 20 October 2026).
- **Film feature** with a short description and "Watch film".
- Heavy JavaScript and an animated reveal on load.

## What works for Seeds

- **Rigour you can see:** content sorted by medium and by type, consistently labelled. Readers always know what kind of thing they're looking at.
- **A strong editorial statement** near the top, saying why the publication exists.
- **Multimedia handled calmly:** reading, listening and watching side by side.
- **Print edition as a first-class object** with price and pre-order, which is relevant to the Seeds magazine.
- Typewriter labels add a slight "document" or archival feel.

## What doesn't fit

- Sleek and clean, close to a luxury aesthetic; nothing muddy or handmade.
- Nature-mystical photography leans towards the woo-woo end.
- The sans-serif, white look is generic for contemporary magazines.
- Heavy loading animation risks content not appearing (it didn't in testing).
