# Feel

Every colour, face and layout decision is judged against one phrase, not against taste. Part of the [SoR design system](README.md); start building from [BUILD.md](BUILD.md). Who we are and how we sound: [brand.md](../2026-sor-website/brand.md).

## Grounded hope

Hope stays the destination, and it is earned by going through the dark rather than around it. A glowing sunrise painting is hope without ground; Dark Mountain is ground without much hope. We sit between: a skilled, caring presence at an ending and at a birth (the doula is a candidate guiding image, still open).

**Light: dawn-leaning.** We are more about renaissance than hospicing an ending, though we do both.

## Three supporting notes

1. **Deep sourcing.** Going to the source, and the creativity that comes from there. Depth with the aim of really understanding.
2. **Crafted rigour.** Real thought, love and care in how things are made; truthfulness rather than being right. The elegance of a potter's bowl, not of Vogue.
3. **Liminal integration.** Not opposites side by side but the moment they become one: dawn, birth, death. Crisis and renaissance as one transition.

## Character

**A little off-centre.** Earthy, a bit muddy, a bit quirky, a bit marginal. Not mainstream, and not a wild artist's site either. The muddiness lives in **texture and imperfection** (paper grain, torn edges, woodcuts, fingerprints), never in muddy colour or raw imagery.

**Nothing may look backwards.** We are seeding a new renaissance, not restaging the old one. Antique-looking type and sepia nostalgia are out.

## What we are not

- Wellness, spiritual woo-woo, new age ("the age of Aquarius")
- Luxury-clean, glossy, fashion-elegant
- Corporate, mainstream, hard selling
- Sleek and contemporary
- Chaotic or self-indulgent like many artist sites

## Rigour and warmth

**Rigour is the form, warmth is the surface** (the potter's bowl). Rigour is constant on every page: grid, type, accuracy, sourcing, care. Warmth is a layer that varies: people, texture, hand marks, colour, voice. Rigour first, but both always present.

| Register | Pages | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| **Study** | White papers, essays, research | Rigour leads; warmth in small touches |
| **Conversation** | Manifesto, podcast, magazine, landing pages | Balanced |
| **Gathering** | Community, events, joining | Warmth leads; rigour stays in the structure |
