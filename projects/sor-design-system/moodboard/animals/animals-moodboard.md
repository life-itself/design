# Animals mood board

Woodcuts, linocuts and engravings of animals, collected for Seeds of Renaissance. Elements cut from these live in [[system/animals/README|system/animals]]. See also [[moodboard/woodcuts/woodcuts-moodboard]].

## 01 Fox sitting, linocut

![[images/01-fox-linocut.png]]

A fox sitting upright and looking up, in solid black with a white chest, ear and tail tip left uncut. A few stray gouge marks around it. Grey paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 02 Two foxes walking, engraving

![[images/02-foxes-walking-engraving.png]]

Two foxes trotting one after the other in fine broken line. Small, watermarked stock preview (vintageretroantique), so draft only. Source link: not yet given.

## 03 Swan with open wings, linocut

![[images/03-swan-wings-linocut.png]]

A swan with an S-curved neck and wings spread, solid black with white cut feather lines. Source link: not yet given.

## 04 Bird carrying a flowering stem, linocut

![[images/04-bird-flowering-stem-linocut.png]]

A blackbird diving with wings raised, carrying a long looping stem with small bell flowers in its beak. Solid black with fine grain in the wings and tail, on clean white. Source link: not yet given.

## 05 Snail, wood engraving

![[images/05-snail-engraving.png]]

A snail reaching out with its horns, the shell in fine engraved lines spiralling inwards and the body stippled. Cream paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 06 Barn owl in a frame, woodcut

![[images/06-barn-owl-framed-woodcut.png]]

A barn owl standing on a rock, face-on, inside a double-ruled frame and signed "H·R". Strong outline with short flecked cuts for feathers. Grey-brown paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 07 Barn owl in flight, rubber stamp

![[images/07-barn-owl-flying-stamp.png]]

A barn owl seen from above, wings swept wide, in fine pen-like line with dotted feathers. Printed on a wooden stamp block. Source link: not yet given.

## 08 Bear standing, woodcut

![[images/08-bear-standing-woodcut.png]]

A bear up on its hind legs, looking up, in solid black with the wood grain showing as fine white streaks through the fur. White paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 09 Wild boar, linocut

![[images/09-wild-boar-linocut.png]]

A wild boar trotting, solid black with rows of short white cuts for its bristly coat, a spiky ridge along the back and a curled tail. White paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 10 Swallow in flight, linocut

![[images/10-swallow-flight-linocut.png]]

A swallow swooping, wings wide and tail forked, solid black with white cut feather lines and a speckled head. Short gouge marks fly out around it like motion. White paper. Close to the swallow already used on the website mockups. Source link: not yet given.

## 11 Hedgehog curled up asleep, linocut

![[images/11-hedgehog-curled-linocut.png]]

A hedgehog curled into an oval, asleep, its face and paws tucked in. Hundreds of fine white spine cuts sweep round the black body. Cream paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 12 Coiled snake, watercolour

![[images/12-snake-coiled-watercolour.png]]

A snake rising from a double coil, painted in dark green-grey scales over a pale yellow belly. A natural history watercolour rather than a print, with a plate number ("79") in the corner. Source link: not yet given.

## 13 Six butterflies, photographic plate

![[images/13-butterflies-six.png]]

Six butterflies in two columns, in soft black and grey on cream paper, like an old natural history plate: a dark birdwing with spotted hindwings, a striped swallowtail, a pale butterfly with one tail, a pale one with dark edges, a banded one and a spotted one. Faint script lettering on the left edge and across the last butterfly. Source link: not yet given.

## 14 Loon swimming, linocut

![[images/14-loon-swimming-linocut.png]]

A loon on the water, its back a grid of small white cuts, a striped collar and white streaks along the flank, with curved strokes for the ripples. White paper. Source link: not yet given.
