# Reference: Dark Mountain Project

- URL: https://dark-mountain.net
- Studied: 2026-10-01 (home page, desktop and narrow widths)
- Status: **liked by the team.** The strongest website reference so far.

## Summary

A literary, handmade, almost monochrome site. Texture and printmaking carry the feel. Dark and light sections meet at torn ink edges. Grounded and serious, but about endings, with little warmth.

## Observed details

### Type
- **One serif for nearly everything:** Libre Baskerville (body 18px; h1 39px; h2 31px; h3 20px; all weight 400, never bold).
- **A small sans for details:** Libre Franklin (meta, dates, small labels).
- Headings are large and light; hierarchy comes from size and space, not weight.

### Colour
| Use | Value |
|---|---|
| Ink (text, dark sections) | `#262623` |
| Dark section variant | `#2e2e2d` |
| Paper (light sections) | `#f4f3f0` |
| Text on dark | `#f3f1ee` |
| Muted links and meta | `#aba8a4` |
| Hero quote | warm amber/orange, overlaid on the illustration |

Colour almost entirely comes from images. The interface itself is ink and paper.

### Texture and material
- The body background is a **paper-grain texture image** (`bg-texture@2x.jpg`, WordPress theme "molecular").
- The sticky dark header ends in a **torn, brushed ink edge** rather than a straight line. The same edge separates dark and light sections further down.
- The logo is an **ink blot or torn-paper shape** with the name set in serif beside it.

### Imagery
- Hero: a full-bleed **black-and-white engraving** of a black-tailed jackrabbit (by Briony Morrow-Cribbs), with a literary quote from the magazine set over it.
- Throughout: woodcuts, linocuts, **papercuts** (two boars in an arch), and raw, slightly strange photography (a person at a table underwater; a body with fur growing from it).
- Nothing glowing, polished or "spiritual-looking". The strangeness is graphic and literary.

### Structure and details
- Navigation items carry small superscript numbers (01 About, 02 Books, 03 Events, 04 Shop, 05 Get Involved), then Subscribe, a basket and a menu.
- Section titles are **rotated vertically** in the left margin, with a small hand-drawn icon (a feather, a book).
- Sections: Latest news, From Our Latest Books, Featured Mountaineer, Featured Section, Dark Kitchen.
- Cards alternate between **dark ink fills and paper fills** in the same row.
- Numbered lists (02, 03) in dark sections; a thin outlined "See all" button sitting on a horizontal rule.
- A long home page (about 8,800px at desktop width).

## What works for Seeds

- **The hand as texture:** paper grain, ink, printmaking. This is a strong model for "a bit muddy" and "imperfection".
- **Material thresholds:** dark and light meet at a torn or brushed edge. This is the version of the Night to Dawn idea that works; a digital gradient does not.
- **Handmade imagery avoids woo-woo:** engraving and printmaking can carry myth and depth without glowing.
- **Restraint in colour:** ink and paper, with colour coming from the images.
- **Literary seriousness:** one serif, light weights, generous size.
- **Quirky structural details** (rotated labels, small hand-drawn icons) give it character without chaos.

## What doesn't fit

- Its subject is an ending ("uncivilisation"). The mood is heavy and lacks the hope and community Seeds needs.
- Some parts feel dated and busy (the amber quote over the hero, dense card rows).
- Little sense of people gathering, joy or warmth.
