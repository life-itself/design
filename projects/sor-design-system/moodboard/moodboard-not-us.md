# Mood board: not us

Images that are **not** us: things we don't want the brand to look or feel like. Kept separate from [[moodboard]] so the two don't get confused. Context is in the user's own words where given. Images are stored in `assets/` with the rest.

## Photo: yoga class on a lake pavilion under a bright blue sky

![[notus-photo-yoga-class-on-lake-pavilion-bright-blue-sky.webp]]

- A bright, saturated colour photograph of a group sitting on yoga mats on a wooden platform with a bamboo roof, facing an instructor, with a lake, green hills and a blue sky with white clouds beyond.
- Context from the user: none given yet, only that this is a "not us" image.
- Noticing: this is polished wellness-retreat imagery, with bright, high-saturation colour, a framed picturesque view, sportswear and an aspirational lifestyle feel. It stands against the grainy, muted, hand-made images on the main [[moodboard]].

## Website: Dragonfruit Media, "Become a top 1% brand on YouTube."

![[notus-screenshot-dragonfruit-media-top-1-percent-youtube-brand.webp]]

- Source: https://www.dragonfruitmedia.co/
- A screenshot of an agency homepage hero. A blurred red and white gradient fills the frame, with a large white serif headline, "Become a top 1% brand on YouTube.", a "Reserve your spot" pill button reading "2 slots left for October", and a row of client logos along the bottom (e.l.f., Y Combinator, Linktree, Flow Research Collective, Maven, ClickUp, GymLaunch).

## Website: Sereth, "Brand stewardship for organizations who give a crap."

![[notus-screenshot-sereth-brand-stewardship-for-orgs-who-give-a-crap.webp]]

- A screenshot of a brand agency's homepage. A blue announcement bar at the top reads "Snag our Website Prep Guide now available in our shop." Below it, a cream page with a blue script logo ("sereth"), small-caps monospaced navigation (Work, Services, Resources, About, Inquire) and a large blue serif headline, "Brand stewardship for organizations who give a crap.", with "give a crap" in italic. A large photo below shows a laptop on a table displaying a client site (Global Doula Project) with a smiling featured recipient's portrait and testimonial text.

- My own observations: it is warmer and more human than the Dragonfruit page above, with a cream and blue palette, an elegant serif and a real client portrait, but it is still an agency selling its services (the shop banner, the Inquire button and the case study).

## Website: Perspectiva, Systems, Souls, Society

![[notus-screenshot-perspectiva-systems-souls-society-neon-wet-street.webp]]

- Source: https://systems-souls-society.com/
- A screenshot of the homepage. It is sleek and contemporary, and it reads as corporate and without personality, even though the subject is deeper. This is the opposite of the grainy, hand-made, aged main [[moodboard]]. Its glowing colour overlaps a little with some main-board images, but the finish is digital and slick.

## Website: Plum Village, "What is happening"

![[notus-screenshot-plum-village-what-is-happening-golden-light-arms-raised.webp]]

- Source: https://plumvillage.org/
- A screenshot of the news page. A large central photo shows a figure seen from behind with arms raised, standing in golden sunset light beside a smaller child, with a "Book Extract / Our Original Nature" headline in brown serif underneath. On the left are a photo of a retreat group lined up on a lawn below a mountain and a soft illustrated scene of trees with roots. A side column advertises "Upcoming Days of Mindfulness".
- Context from the user: the website is "not us". The user said the real organisation may be better than this, but the site communicates a certain feel.
- Why it's not us (the user's words): "a bit too woo-woo alternative, like feel good", such as the picture of "people rising to the sun". It reads as wellness, "and we're not that". There is "no acknowledgement of the shadow", "no rigor" and "no depth". It's "just feel good. And meditate", "happy, clappy". They added that "overall, it may not be that, but it communicates that."
- Summary: the sunlit, arms-raised imagery and the feel-good tone signal uncritical positivity, with no shadow and no rigour. The point is about what it communicates, not necessarily what the organisation is. This is the clearest statement so far of the wellness line, together with the Perspectiva essay grid, and it connects to the yoga class. The main [[moodboard]] keeps the dark and the difficult in view, as in the first collage, where the sun comes through grey clouds.

## Photo: Plum Village t-shirt with a gold Zen circle, seen from behind

![[notus-photo-plum-village-black-tshirt-gold-zen-circle-back-view.webp]]

- Source: a Plum Village t-shirt (see the [[#Website: Plum Village, "What is happening"|Plum Village website entry]] above).
- A photograph of a person from behind, wearing a plain black t-shirt with a large gold brush-stroke circle (an enso) and a small red seal stamp printed on the back, with grey jeans. The setting is a warm, softly blurred hall with stone walls and golden fabric.
- Why it's not us (the user's words): it makes them "feel very uncomfortable". It has "no style really", "unelegant". It is Zen "turned into a logo", and there's a danger of turning a thing like this into merchandise. It feels "a bit mass" and "very hippie". They described a hippie style that is almost caring about how you look, but in an unaesthetic way, with baggy clothes and a "lack of care". Their objection is that it shows "almost a lack of rigor in how they would dress".
- Summary: the problem isn't the circle itself. It's the merchandise treatment and the casual, unrefined hippie look, which reads as a lack of care and rigour. This is the opposite of the hand-made, carefully made images on the main [[moodboard]], such as the [[painting-red-brush-calligraphy-character-on-cream|calligraphy]], which is a real brush gesture and not a logo.
- Left out on purpose: the user's remarks about the model's physical appearance. The point is about the styling and the merch, and I haven't recorded personal comments about a real person.
