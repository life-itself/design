# Mountains mood board

Blue pen-and-ink mountain drawings, collected as a possible Seeds of Renaissance sub-brand. Elements cut from these live in [[system/mountains/README|system/mountains]].

## 01 High peak, blue pen

![[images/01-high-peak-pen-blue.webp]]

One steep peak in dense ballpoint hatching, wisps of cloud, pines in the foreground. Clean white paper, so it cuts out well. Source link: not yet given.

## 02 Ridge, ultramarine brush

![[images/02-ridge-brush-ultramarine.webp]]

A loose ridge in bold ultramarine brush strokes, with a paler watery wash on the right. Cream paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 03 Peak on grid paper

![[images/03-peak-grid-blue.webp]]

A tall peak above a band of mist, in fine blue lines on cream grid paper. Light at the base, dense navy at the top. Source link: not yet given.

## 04 Small peak, violet pen

![[images/04-small-peak-pen-violet.png]]

A compact peak in quick violet-blue ballpoint hatching on white. Source link: not yet given.

## 05 Range with contour lines

![[images/05-range-contours-pen-navy.webp]]

A whole range seen from above, in navy pen on cream, with map-like contour lines drifting through the sky. Source link: not yet given.

## 06 Ridge, 26 Sept 1934

![[images/06-ridge-1934-pen-blue.webp]]

A ridge sketched on the spot in parallel hatching, signed "26 sept 34, 6h soir, Plateau de Paris". Faded blue ink on pale paper. Source link: not yet given.

## 07 Peaks and waves, linocut on map

![[images/07-peaks-waves-linocut-map.webp]]

Bold black linocut of jagged peaks above curling waves, printed over an old topographic map. Already roughly cut out in the source. Source link: not yet given.
