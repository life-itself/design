Seeds of Renaissance · logo · chosen 13 Oct 2026

Seeds of Renaissance logo set

Version #4: the swallow 30% bigger with a cut-and-printed edge, prints pushed a little with the bird in front. Step 1 is every colourway of the mark on the brand's grounds. Step 2 sets it with the wordmark: Polyamine capitals with a smaller italic "of", adjustable live.

1. The mark in every colourway

Five colourways tried on the five grounds the brand uses. Green marks a pairing to use, red one to avoid. Now sharp: the bird is a vector shape and the fingerprints come from the 1254 px original. Files: examples/logo/final/img/mark-*.svg and .png (1130 px, @2x 2260 px).

2. Final lockups on every ground

Your saved "Final version" settings, made into fixed files: the capitals and the "of" are outlines, the mark is the full #4. Each lockup comes in five colourways. Green marks a pairing to use, red one to avoid. Files: examples/logo/final/lockups/ (SVG, plus PNG at 1200 and 2400 px wide, transparent).

3. The mark at every size

The full mark, every fingerprint, at every size: no simplified versions. Shown at real pixel size. Files: examples/logo/final/img/sizes/ (16 to 1024 px, @2x up to 128), plus favicon.ico.

4. Mark and wordmark: the bench

The capitals are Polyamine, drawn as shapes, with its own kerning. The "of" is Fraunces italic, so its size, weight and position can be adjusted here. Change the sliders until it looks right, then copy the settings into chat and I'll fix them in the final files.
