# Seeds of Renaissance brand: status

*Updated 2 October 2026*

**Where we are:** the feel is agreed (**Grounded hope**) and a first design system is in place: ink, white, paper and pale yellow, torn edges, woodcut graphics, black buttons and red links. It has been applied to six website pages and four YouTube thumbnails. Next come photography, the logo, slides and print.

## The design system in brief

| | |
|---|---|
| **Feel** | Grounded hope. Deep sourcing, crafted rigour, liminal integration. A little off-centre. |
| **Not** | Wellness or new age, luxury, corporate, hard selling, sleek and contemporary |
| **Colours** | White and paper `#fbfaf6` grounds · pale yellow `#fbefc4` as background only · ink `#1b1916` for text and **all buttons** · one night section `#171613` per page · logo red `#E5201C` **only for occasional links** |
| **Type** | Polyamine (headings), Bricolage Grotesque (text), Appful Grotesque (labels), soft italic serif (poetry) |
| **Graphics** | Torn edges, woodcut swallows and seeds, figures captioned like plates, logo on a white disc |

More detail: [design-system-plan.md](design-system-plan.md)
