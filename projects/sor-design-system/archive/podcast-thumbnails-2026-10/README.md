# Podcast thumbnails, October 2026

How the podcast thumbnail was arrived at, 12–13 October 2026. Superseded by the chosen design: swallows and fingerprints from the bottom left, built as the [podcast thumbnail Canva kit](../../examples/youtube/canva-kit/README.md) and the [Instagram kit](../../examples/social/instagram/README.md). Overview of the chosen look: [Swallows & Fingerprints](https://claude.ai/artifact/MMNCAeF3gNzmpDSiB6aVDv) (Claude artifact; shows the logo before 13 October).

| File | What it was |
|---|---|
| [brief.md](brief.md) | The mini-project brief, with the thumbnail research summary and the progress log |
| [versions-a-to-d.html](versions-a-to-d.html) | First round: A face-led, B quote-led, C background-led, D colour-coded, in mocked YouTube feeds |
| [backgrounds.html](backgrounds.html) | Sixteen background treatments and the "dawn ridge" (2 + 10) combinations, with notes on what makes a good background |
| [swallows-versions.html](swallows-versions.html) | The swallows on white: versions 1–11 (black, blue, two speakers, blue words, fingerprints). Version 8 was chosen, then moved to the bottom left |
| `grove/` | The Grove covers (speaker in the seed spiral on sunlight), from before this round. Its PNG exports are not committed; `grove/export.sh` rebuilds them |
| `workshop.html` | A thumbnail workshop page with a field panel, from before this round |

The pages here use the old logo file names, so they now show the new mark; the old fingerprint backgrounds in them are approximate.
