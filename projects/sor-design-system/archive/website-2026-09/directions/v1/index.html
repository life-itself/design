Seeds of Renaissance · website design · directions draft 1 · 28 Sept 2026

Four directions for the new Seeds of Renaissance site

Each direction is shown as a home page and the opening of the manifesto, using real copy, real courses and gatherings, and photos from the current site. These are first sketches to react to, not finished designs. Fonts and colours are fresh proposals: nothing is carried over from the old 2R site or the Canva deck.

Fixed

Vision and movement for civilisational renewal. Movement and community first; media is the way in. Home pages lead with people, gatherings and ways to take part.

Open

Everything visual: type, colour, layout, imagery, motion, and the mark. The references (Emergence, Openhouse, ECC, Cargo sites, Lee Holmes) informed fonts and feel only.

Look for

  • Which one feels most like us?
  • Does the home page feel like joining a movement?
  • Can the manifesto's voice live here?

Manifesto

Seeds of Renaissance Manifesto

A Manifesto for Civilizational Renewal

For all the living beings whose heart burns to see a new world emerge.

This manifesto is non-sectarian. It is to be alive and not to be set in stone, dead and irrelevant. It is a reverence and celebration of life. It expresses patterns and guiding principles, like a common DNA, from which evolutions may arise.

We are a movement called to bring forth a renaissance.We are seed-bearers pioneering a world that works for everyone.We create a new world through our work.We are the bridge between the dying past and the future unborn.

So what will be this Renaissance we are seeding? It is yet to be known but we answer to the call of a dawn, calling us to value interbeingness, awakening to the wisdom of the whole, inner-evolution and dancing with the richness of complexity.

Life as an art.
In this soil we plant the seeds of a Renaissance.

        Direction E · added on request

        Warm Emergence

        Close to Emergence Magazine in layout and pace: a centred wordmark, full-bleed feature modules, a short statement of belief, a list of selected work labelled by form, and the print issue shown as an object. It is made warmer with a soft vintage display serif in the spirit of Restora, and led by gatherings rather than stories.

        Fraunces (light, soft) stands in for Restora, which is a paid font that can’t be loaded here. With a Restora web licence it can be embedded directly.

        Manifesto

        A Manifesto for Civilizational Renewal

        Seeds of Renaissance

        For all the living beings whose heart burns to see a new world emerge.

        Human civilization has always been a story of transformation, of old worlds dying and new ones being born. We are living through such moments. The crises of our time, ecological, political, social and spiritual, are not separate emergencies to be managed one by one. They are the symptoms of a civilization whose foundations are cracking. And as in every great turning, crisis carries within it the seed of rebirth.

        We are a movement called to bring forth a renaissance.We are seed-bearers pioneering a world that works for everyone.We create a new world through our work.We are the bridge between the dying past and the future unborn.

        So what will be this Renaissance we are seeding? It is yet to be known but we answer to the call of a dawn, calling us to value interbeingness, awakening to the wisdom of the whole, inner-evolution and dancing with the richness of complexity.

        Life as an art. In this soil we plant the seeds of a Renaissance.

          Direction C

          Honest Workshop, used as a pattern

          Plain and warm, like a well-kept notebook. On its own it loses the beauty and the myth, so rather than a separate identity it is folded into every direction as the way practical offers are shown.

          From ECC: who, what, when, cost and how to join, stated plainly, with a participant’s words as the emotional moment. You can see it in each “Coming up” list and quote above.

          Questions for review

          1. Which direction feels most like Seeds of Renaissance? Which feels least like it?
          2. Does any home page make you feel you are joining a movement, not subscribing to a publication?
          3. Is there a hybrid you would want, such as A’s warmth with D’s dusk-to-dawn structure, or A with B’s blackletter as a signature?
          4. For the manifesto: should it look like a book, a poster, or a night sky?
          5. What is missing? The biggest gap is photographs of people together, and of Sylvie and Rufus.
