Long-form reading.reading with .drop, .pull, .verse and .litany; a seal to close

Human civilization has always been a story of transformation, of old worlds dying and new ones being born. We are living through such moments. The crises of our time are not separate emergencies to be managed one by one.

We are a movement called to bring forth a renaissance.We are the bridge between the dying past and the future unborn.

We gather once a month on a Global Connection Call, open to anyone who feels this call too.

  1. Our work is a practice of hope, because where there is hope there is action.
  2. Our work is contemplative and activist at the same time.
  3. Our work is life as an art.
