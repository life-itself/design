The diagram signature

Every published figure carries this signature, bottom right inside its margin, so it stays attributed when it is cropped and reshared. Rules: diagrams.md. Files: system/img/signature.svg and signature-dark.svg, with PNGs and an Excalidraw scene.

  • Sized for a 1600px-wide export: mark 64px, text 20px, 72px margin.
  • Light grounds: the mark on its white disc. Dark grounds: the inverted mark, no disc (provisional until the inverted mark is designed).
  • The URL is a placeholder (seeds-domain.tbd/wisdom) until the domain is decided.
