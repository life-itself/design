# Experiences

Candidate experiences, distilled from [raw/2026-09-29-first-outflow-experiences-vision.md](raw/2026-09-29-first-outflow-experiences-vision.md). Each is a self-contained, animated piece. Once one is chosen for development it gets its own folder (`experiences/NN-slug/`) with brief, script and moodboard.

## 0. Home page (integration)

Joins the parts (Arrival, Dawn, Leap, Book, Flight) into one page and writes the frame contract → [experiences/00-home/HANDOFF.md](experiences/00-home/HANDOFF.md).

## 1. Arrival: the seed

Now the first part of the home page ([experiences/01-arrival/](experiences/01-arrival/HANDOFF.md)); the whole-page brief is [experiences/00-home/brief.md](experiences/00-home/brief.md).

Full dark or full white void. Text emerges: a quote "for the one whose heart longs for…". Scroll: a seed symbol spins on itself and starts to bloom. A manifesto quote explains what SoR is. Each word ("Wisdom", "Interbeing", …) becomes a different tree that blooms.

## 2. Courses: the pop-up book

Brief → [experiences/04-book/brief.md](experiences/04-book/brief.md). Part 4 of the home page; now a Kiefer-like lead book with rising gold letters (pop-ups parked).

A book you open. Pages reveal the courses as things that pop up out of the page: the Zen monastery, the lay monastic path, etc. Playful, no care for conventional navigation.

## 3. Movement: the leap

Brief → [experiences/03-leap/brief.md](experiences/03-leap/brief.md). Part 3 of the home page.

A photograph (the Magnum image). Little people gradually walk into it, through darkness towards possibility. As they walk in, the image rips open: a leap of faith. Then "Come join our call", rip, "Enjoy".

## 4. Temple of the sacred world

A church/temple that decays into nature: stones remain, trees and birds grow through. The temple is still there, now natural. Enter it and meet big type: "Listening to birds", "Prayer for the perpetrator". You can listen to each.
