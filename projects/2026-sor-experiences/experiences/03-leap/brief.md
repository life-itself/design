# Movement: the Leap — brief (draft)

Status: in progress, 2026-09-29. Used as **frame 3** of the [Arrival](../01-arrival/brief.md) landing page; can also stand alone (e.g. on a join/movement page). Source: [raw/2026-09-29-first-outflow-experiences-vision.md](../../raw/2026-09-29-first-outflow-experiences-vision.md).

## Feeling

Walking through darkness into possibility. A leap of faith. The invitation to join the movement.

## Source image

[moodboard/leap-collage-walkers-torn-field.png](moodboard/leap-collage-walkers-torn-field.png). A black-and-white photo collage: a line of people in dark coats walk away from us along a road into heavy grey clouds. Ahead of them, a torn-paper hole opens onto colour: a yellow flower field under blue sky. The road leads straight into the tear.

**The dream: animate this image itself.** Use the people and scene from the picture, not new illustrations.

## Script (sketch)

Scroll-driven.

1. **Darkness.** The grey scene, clouds and road, with no tear yet. The people stand at the near end.
2. **Walking.** As you scroll, the people walk up the road into the clouds, one by one.
3. **The rip.** As they reach the far end, the paper tears open like paper (rough white fibrous edge, as in the image) to reveal the yellow field and blue sky. This is the "ding" moment: grey to colour.
4. **Join.** "Come join our call" → another rip → "Enjoy". The join offer (the meetup) sits here; wording and details to be supplied, may be general.
5. **Onward.** A "keep learning" path leads on to the Book (frame 4).

## Craft notes

- The tear is paper: torn edge, white fibres, slight depth/shadow.
- No logo. What's behind the tear is the field from the image.
- Cutting out the figures and layering them (parallax, walk cycles) is the main production task.

## Open questions

- Join offer: wording, link, prominence.
- Words beyond "Come join our call" / "Enjoy".
- Does colour spill out of the tear into the rest of the frame, or stay contained?
