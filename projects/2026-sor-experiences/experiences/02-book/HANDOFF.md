# Frame 4: The Book — handoff

Frame 4 of the Arrival page ([Arrival brief](../01-arrival/brief.md), [all handoffs](../01-arrival/HANDOFFS.md)); can also stand alone on a courses/learning page. Full brief: [brief.md](brief.md).

Suggested model: **Opus 5.5**. The book itself may be best made as 3D (Three.js) or motion graphics (After Effects exported to video/Lottie), so it can land closed and heave open.

## Goal

A heavy Anselm Kiefer-like lead book falls out of the dark, opens, and gold letters rise from its pages to spell what we offer.

## Feeling

Weight, age, treasure: decades of hard-won learning, offered up. Magical, not informational. First priority: the book's beauty and its arrival.

## Beats (scroll-driven)

1. **The fall.** Black space. The book drops in heavily from the top and lands. Ideal: lands closed, then heaves itself open. Fallback: lands open.
2. **Illumination.** Gold, illuminated-manuscript letters glow on the open pages.
3. **Rising.** The letters lift off, float up and assemble above the book into **COURSES · PAPERS**. They stay.
4. **Caption** below the book.

No page turning. Don't write onto the pages themselves (it looks fake); the letters rise into clean black space.

## Text

- **COURSES · PAPERS** (each a link, destinations to come)
- *Sharing what we have learned on our journey over the past decades.*
