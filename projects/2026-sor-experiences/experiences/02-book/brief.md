# Courses: the Book — brief (draft)

Used as **frame 4** of the [Arrival](../01-arrival/brief.md) landing page; can also stand alone (e.g. on a courses/learning page). Sources: [first outflow](../../raw/2026-09-29-first-outflow-experiences-vision.md), [book detail](../../raw/2026-09-29-book-detail.md).

## Feeling

Weight, age, treasure. Hard-won knowledge from decades of journey, offered up. Magical rather than informational. First focus: the book itself, its beauty and its arrival.

## Reference

[moodboard/kiefer-lead-book-open.webp](moodboard/kiefer-lead-book-open.webp): an Anselm Kiefer lead book, open, heavy rippled pages, oxidised patina (rust red, verdigris blue, pale yellow) on black. **Open: animate the photo itself, or recreate?** Animating the photo (fair use) may be easier; recreating in motion graphics / 3D gives more control (closed → open). It need not match exactly.

## Script

Scroll-driven (assumed, consistent with other frames).

| Beat | What you see | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The fall | Black space. The book **drops in heavily from the top** and lands. Ideal: it lands closed, then heaves itself open. Fallback: it lands already open. | |
| 2. Illumination | Golden, illuminated-manuscript letters begin to glow on the open pages. | |
| 3. Rising | The letters lift off the pages, float up and **assemble in the black space above the book** into a few words. They stay there. | **Courses** · **Papers** (3–6 items max; categories, not individual titles; final list to confirm) |
| 4. Caption | Below the book. | *Sharing what we have learned on our journey over the past decades.* |

No page turning. Writing onto the pages directly would look fake, hence the letters rise into clean space.

## Craft notes

- Book: 3D or high-end motion graphics. Lead texture, weight on landing (settle, slight bounce, dust?).
- Gold lettering: illuminated-manuscript feel, glowing; readable once assembled.
- Assembled words are the links (to courses, to papers).
