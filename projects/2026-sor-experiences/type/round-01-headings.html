Seeds of Renaissance · type · round one · reviewed 30 September 2026

Heading faces, and what was said about them

Nineteen candidates for the Dawn statement, shown in the real frame. This is the record of the round, kept so a later one does not re-propose what has already been turned down. Two were liked: Polyamine and Restora.

What came out of it

“Weightiness is important, this grounded weightiness. We are saying something relatively significant, but at the same time it wants to have a bit of an edge, a bit of novelty. It wants life — life and groundedness and wholeness — without becoming just organic kitsch.”

“I end up liking Restora and Polyamine because they have something a little organic and elegant, but they are both fonts that do feel quite luxurious and sleek. The thing is, if you take that, then you have to compensate with something more weighty and grounded in the body.”

The rejections divide cleanly. Most of the classical faces failed as boring — well drawn, no personality, nothing new. The stranger ones failed as backward-looking, which is the sharper objection: this is a new rebirth emerging forwards, not a return to the last Renaissance, so runes and revivals are out.

The full brief, and where it goes next: type.md.

Polyamine

Nasir-style commercial display serif · UICreative

Liked

Organic and elegant. Luxurious and sleek, but with a living quality.

Polyamine we like. It has something a little organic and elegant, but it is also a font that feels quite luxurious and sleek. Quite like luxury, high class, yet at the same time alive — it has this organic, living quality.
