Study deck

For white papers, research talks and seminars: rigour leads. A fifteen-slide talk on Wisdom and Wanting What’s Good (White paper No. 6), as a mock to work out both the design and the talk.

  • Every content slide states a claim as a sentence in Restora, then shows the evidence: a figure, a short list, a table or a quote.
  • Polyamine on the cover only. White ground; woodcuts only on section slides; the one night is the closing slide, as on the paper’s back cover.
  • Figures are the house diagrams, unchanged: they carry their own title and signature. Photographs sit flat and square.
