Gathering deck

For calls, community talks and art evenings: warmth leads. A ten-slide talk built only from the Seeds of Renaissance system, on a fixed 1920 × 1080 artboard.

  • White is the default ground; paper and sunlight sometimes; one night slide at most per section, here the section break for part two.
  • Polyamine for titles under about ten words, Restora for the long quote, Bricolage to read, Apfel Grotezk for labels. Nothing under 28px on the artboard, so it reads from the back of a room.
  • Red only as the logo's own red and one red seed beside the quote. Photos are placeholders.
